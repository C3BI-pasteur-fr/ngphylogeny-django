{% comment %} Shared across every page - used to be a handful of individual pages each rendering their own ad-hoc messages block, some filtering to error-tagged messages only. A message added on a page that never rendered any such block at all (e.g. account.views.AccountDeleteView's own confirmation, which redirects to the home page) used to sit queued (django.contrib.messages defaults to cookie-based storage, unaffected by login/logout/session changes) until it happened to land on one of those few pages later, however much later that was - reported live as a stale "Your account and its analyses have been deleted" message showing up on the account page right after a brand new, unrelated sign-up. Rendering (and thereby consuming) every message on every page, right where it's actually added, is what actually fixes that, not just this specific report of it.
